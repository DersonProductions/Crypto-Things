# Enforcement Guidelines for Code of Conduct

These guidelines outline the process for enforcing the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in the Crypto-Things community. They are designed to ensure fair, consistent, and transparent handling of incidents. Community leaders (e.g., repository maintainers) are responsible for enforcement.

## Scope
These guidelines apply to all community spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other interactions related to the project.

## Reporting Process
- Reports should be submitted via the method specified in the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) or directly to maintainers (e.g., via email or private message).
- All reports will be treated confidentially.

## Investigation
1. **Acknowledgment**: Respond to the reporter within 48 hours to confirm receipt.
2. **Gather Information**: Collect details from involved parties without bias. This may include screenshots, logs, or statements.
3. **Assessment**: Evaluate if the behavior violates the Code of Conduct, considering context and severity.

## Severity Levels
We categorize violations into levels to guide responses:

| Level | Description | Examples |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Low** | Minor infractions that don't harm others. | Off-topic comments, mild sarcasm. |
| **Medium** | Behaviors that disrupt or offend. | Insulting language, repeated off-topic posts. |
| **High** | Serious violations causing harm. | Harassment, threats, doxxing. |

## Corrective Actions
Actions escalate based on severity and history:

- **Warning**: For low-level issues—private or public notice to correct behavior.
- **Temporary Ban**: For medium issues—suspend participation (e.g., close issues/PRs) for 1-7 days.
- **Permanent Ban**: For high-level or repeated issues—remove from community, block on GitHub.
- **Content Removal**: Delete violating comments/commits.

Leaders will document decisions and communicate them to affected parties.

## Appeals
- Affected individuals can appeal within 7 days by contacting another leader.
- Appeals are reviewed impartially; decisions are final.

## Transparency
- Annual summary of enforcement actions (anonymized) may be shared.
- Avoid public discussion of specific cases to protect privacy.

## Updates
These guidelines may be updated. Changes will be announced in the repo.

If you're a leader, enforce with empathy and fairness. For questions, discuss in maintainer channels.
